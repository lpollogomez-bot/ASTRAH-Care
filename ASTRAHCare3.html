🥗 SPACE NUTRITION REFERENCE (NASA)NASA requirements

Figures come from NASA exploration nutrition requirements (JSC-67378), NASA-STD-3001 and the ISS requirements table. Individual needs vary; this is not a personal prescription.

🔥 Energy

≈2,300–3,050 kcal/dayNASA-STD-3001 mission average 3,035 kcal; about 3,041 (men, avg 82.9 kg) and 2,344 (women, avg 65.1 kg) with an active factor. Add 200 kcal per EVA hour.

🥩 Protein

1.2–1.8 g/kg/dayExploration requirement (JSC-67378). The older ISS table lists 12–15% of energy.

🥑 Fat

30–35% of energyISS requirements table. Fish intake was associated with less bone loss in ISS crews.

💊 Vitamin D & Sodium

Vit. D 25 µg (1,000 IU)Daily requirement; vitamin D status often drops in flight. Sodium is monitored because space diets tend to be high in it.
